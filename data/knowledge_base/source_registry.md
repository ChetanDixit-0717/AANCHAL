## Source management rules

1. Every knowledge document must have a unique source ID.
2. Record the original publisher and publication year.
3. Record the official source URL.
4. Record age-group applicability.
5. Record licensing and reuse restrictions.
6. Check for newer guidance before treating recommendations as current.
7. Keep historical statistics separate from current statistics.
8. Review factual content before including it in the chatbot's retrieval corpus.

## Review status definitions

- Candidate: Identified but not reviewed.
- Curated: Summary prepared from the source.
- Verified: Content and applicability reviewed.
- Restricted: Reuse permission or licence needs clarification.
- Retired: Superseded or no longer suitable for retrieval.
